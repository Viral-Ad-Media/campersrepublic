The first RV trip usually teaches you what you forgot. This checklist covers the gear that experienced RVers keep on board, along with a walk-around to do before every departure.

## Hookups

**Electric**
- Surge protector or electrical management system
- Power cord adapters (for example, 30-to-50 amp and 30-to-15 amp "dogbone" adapters)
- Heavy-duty extension cord rated for RV use

**Water**
- Drinking-water-safe hose (usually white or blue), plus a spare
- Water pressure regulator
- Inline water filter
- Hose elbow and a Y-splitter

**Sewer**
- Sewer hose (with a spare) and fittings
- Sewer hose support
- Disposable gloves
- Tank treatment and RV-safe toilet paper
- Rinse hose kept separate from your drinking-water hose

## Leveling and stability

- Leveling blocks or ramps
- Wheel chocks
- Stabilizer jack pads
- A bubble level or a level app

## Kitchen

- Cookware that fits your stove and oven
- Plates, mugs and utensils (unbreakable is best)
- Non-slip shelf liner and tension rods to stop items shifting
- Coffee maker or kettle
- Outdoor grill or camp stove for cooking outside
- Dish soap, sponges, towels and trash bags
- Paper towels and food storage containers

## Bathroom and bedroom

- Towels and bath mats
- Toiletries and a first aid kit
- Bedding sized for your RV mattress
- Extra blankets for cold nights

## Outdoor living

- Camp chairs and a folding table
- Outdoor rug or mat
- Lanterns and string lights
- Awning tie-downs
- Portable fire pit if the site allows it

## Safety

- Smoke and carbon monoxide detectors (test them each trip)
- Propane leak detector
- Fire extinguishers (one inside, one in an exterior bay)
- Roadside emergency kit with reflective triangles
- Tire pressure gauge (and a monitoring system if you have one)
- Flashlights and spare batteries

## Tools

- Basic tool kit: screwdrivers, wrenches, pliers, socket set
- Cordless drill with a socket that fits your stabilizer jacks
- Duct tape, zip ties and sealant tape for quick fixes
- Spare fuses and bulbs
- Work gloves

## Documents

- Registration and insurance
- Roadside assistance details
- Campground reservations saved offline
- Owner's manuals for the RV and appliances

## Pre-departure walk-around

1. Retract slides, awnings and stabilizer jacks.
2. Close and latch all doors, cabinets, roof vents and windows.
3. Turn off the water pump and water heater, and switch the fridge to its travel setting.
4. Disconnect and stow power, water and sewer hookups.
5. Check that the hitch and safety chains are secure, or that the toad (towed car) is connected.
6. Test brake lights and turn signals.
7. Check tire pressure.
8. Walk all the way around the rig one last time.
